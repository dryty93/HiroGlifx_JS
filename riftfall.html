What is actually working?

23 · Physics: Havok V2 handles rigid bodies and character collision using a capsule controller, with camera obstruction checks.

24 · Assets and animation: a local original glTF file provides idle, walk, run, jump, attack and death clips. If loading fails, the scene explicitly reports a procedural placeholder.

25 · Combat: health, armor, stamina, attack ranges, weapon damage, cooldowns, statuses and hit events share a reusable engine.

26 · NPCs: A* grid navigation and behavior trees choose chase, attack or idle based on HiroGlifx rules.

Complete executable HiroGlifx rules

These rules tune attack damage, combo bonuses, enemy aggression and victory handling. JS supplies the engine, not game-specific balancing decisions.
